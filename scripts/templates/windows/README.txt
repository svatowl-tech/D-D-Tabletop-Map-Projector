========================================================================
  VTT-ZERO Tabletop Map Projector (Windows Portable Edition)
========================================================================

QUICK START:
1. Double click "start-dual-screen.bat" to open both DM Console and Projector window.
   OR double click "launch-dm.bat" to open Master controls.
   OR double click "launch-projector.bat" to open the clean Player view.

2. Move the Player View window to your second screen / TV / Projector.
   Press F11 (or click the bottom-right fullscreen button) to enter fullscreen.

3. On the DM Console, drag & drop your map (JPG, PNG, WebP, MP4, WebM).
   Use fog tools (Reveal [R], Hide [H], Pan [Space], Grid, Zoom).
   Everything synchronizes to the projector with 0-latency via BroadcastChannel!

NO INSTALLATION OR INTERNET REQUIRED. 100% OFFLINE.
